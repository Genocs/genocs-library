The Genocs enterprise microservices suite is deployed as:
- apigateway
- identities
- products
- orders
- notifications

Each service is exposed on port 8080 by default.

To port-forward a service (example for apigateway):

  export POD_NAME=$(kubectl get pods --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l "app=apigateway,release={{ .Release.Name }}" -o jsonpath="{.items[0].metadata.name}")
  kubectl port-forward $POD_NAME 8080:8080
  echo "Visit http://127.0.0.1:8080 to use apigateway"

Repeat for other services by changing the app label.
