Infrastructure dependencies (mongodb, seq, jaeger) are NOT installed by
this chart and must already exist in the cluster. Override their URLs via
values.yaml -> env.* if your addresses differ.

To port-forward the primary demo WebApi:

  export POD_NAME=$(kubectl get pods --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    -l "app={{ .Values.demoWebapi.name }},release={{ .Release.Name }}" -o jsonpath="{.items[0].metadata.name}")
  kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} $POD_NAME 5700:{{ .Values.demoWebapi.containerPort }}
  echo "Visit http://127.0.0.1:5700"
